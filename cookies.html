Cookie Policy

Last updated: January 2026. Understand how we utilize cookies and local browser storage to personalize your browsing experience.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies and browser local storage mechanisms are small data files retained on your client device. They enable our web servers and client scripts to remember your preferences (such as dark mode preferences and active session states).

2. How YoungMark Employs Cookies

  • Essential Preferences: Preserving your visual theme selection (Dark / Light mode).
  • Performance Analytics: Aggregate, anonymized traffic diagnostics to enhance page loading velocity.
  • Session Security: Validating anti-CSRF tokens and inquiry form payloads.

3. Managing Your Browser Preferences

Most modern browsers permit you to decline or clear cookies via settings. However, disabling essential cookies may alter functionality such as theme retention.

4. Contact

For questions regarding our cookie practices, reach us at privacy@youngmarkglobal.com.
