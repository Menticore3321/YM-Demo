Accounting Workflow

The Financial Data Lifecycle

Our structured accounting cycle guides transactions from raw intake through reconciliation, general ledger posting, and executive board reporting.

1

Transaction Intake

Ingestion of bank feeds, invoices, merchant gateway payouts, and payroll records.

2

Reconciliation

Chartered audit matching, sub-ledger balancing, and accrual schedule adjustments.

3

General Ledger Close

Strict 5-day month-end financial closing with trial balance verification.

4

Executive MIS Decks

Preparation of dynamic board presentations, variance analyses, and rolling forecasts.
