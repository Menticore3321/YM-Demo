Institutional Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. Review how YoungMark Global Advisory manages, safeguards, and maintains confidentiality for all enterprise data.

1. Overview & Commitment to Confidentiality

YoungMark Global Advisory ("YoungMark", "we", "us", or "our") respects the fiduciary trust placed in us by our clients. This Privacy Policy outlines our standards regarding the collection, handling, encryption, and protection of financial records, personal identification data, and business communications.

2. Information We Ingest & Process

In delivering strategic financial consulting, bookkeeping, and cloud systems architecture, we process:

  • Financial Records: General ledgers, trial balances, invoices, payroll registers, and banking feeds authorized via secure API tokens.
  • Corporate Identification: Company tax numbers, entity registrations, and director contact details.
  • Technical Telemetry: Log data, IP addresses, and session timestamps collected during interactions with our client portal.

3. Cryptographic Security & Storage Protocols

All client data at rest is encrypted with AES-256 standard, and data in transit is shielded with TLS 1.3 protocol. Access to financial records is partitioned through strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) and hardware-backed Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA). Under no circumstance is client data sold or used for public machine learning training.

4. International Data Transfers

For multinational clients, data transfers across cross-border cloud regions follow standard contractual clauses (SCCs) in adherence to GDPR, UK DPA, and local data protection regulations.

5. Data Retention & Right to Eradication

We retain financial documentation strictly for statutory periods required by applicable taxation authorities (typically 7–10 years). Upon contract termination or explicit request, non-statutory records are permanently scrubbed from our systems.

6. Privacy Inquiries & Data Protection Officer

For questions concerning this privacy framework or to exercise data subject rights, contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@youngmarkglobal.com.
