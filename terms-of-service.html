2. Professional Advisory Scope

YoungMark provides financial advisory, corporate bookkeeping, fractional CFO consulting, executive MIS reporting, and tax compliance services. While our Chartered Accountants maintain the highest statutory diligence, client leadership retains final responsibility for management decisions and representations made to tax authorities.

The quality of our deliverables relies on the prompt, accurate furnishing of raw financial records, bank access, and operational documentation. Delays in document provisioning may adjust closing schedules and report deliveries.
